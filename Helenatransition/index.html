The process

How the transition works

A small team is running a short, focused process between Election Day and inauguration. Here is what happens after you reach out.

  1. Tell us about yourself

    Share your background, the areas of state government you care about, and a résumé. It takes about five minutes.

  2. The team reviews

    Transition staff read every submission and match people to agency reviews, policy working groups, boards and commissions, and open roles.

  3. We follow up

    If there's a fit, someone from the team will reach out by email to set up a conversation. Timing depends on how each area's work unfolds.

Submitting your information doesn't guarantee a role. It makes sure the team knows you're interested and can find you when the work calls for your experience.

Timeline

From Election Day to day one

  1. Oct

    Transition planning begins

    The team sets up its structure and begins collecting interest from people who want to help RI work better for residents and businesses.

  2. Nov 3

    Election Day

    Rhode Islanders vote for governor.

  3. Nov–Dec

    Agency reviews and priorities

    Working groups review state agencies and shape the first-year agenda. Submissions are matched to this work as it takes shape.

  4. December

    Leadership appointments

    Senior administration roles are announced as they are filled.

  5. Jan 5

    Inauguration

    The new administration takes office at the State House.
